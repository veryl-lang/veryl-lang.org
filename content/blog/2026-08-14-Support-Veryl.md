Veryl is now accepting sponsorships. You can support the project through
[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/veryl-lang) and
[Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/veryl).

Veryl is, and will remain, free and open source. Sponsorships help keep the
project healthy and independent, and let us invest more in its future.

## Where the money goes

All funds are managed transparently through
[Open Source Collective](https://opencollective.com/veryl), our fiscal host, so
the budget is public and open for anyone to inspect.

Today, the running costs are modest: continuous integration, hosting, and
domain renewals for the website, documentation, and the package registry. As the
project grows, we want sponsorships to go further:

- **Development and maintenance**: sustaining the ongoing work of reviewing
  contributions, fixing bugs, and shipping regular releases.
- **Outreach**: presenting Veryl at conferences and meetups to grow its
  community.

## How to support Veryl

- **Individuals** can sponsor through
  [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/veryl-lang) or
  [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/veryl).
- **Companies and organizations** can become a sponsor on
  [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/veryl) or
  [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/veryl-lang), and have their logo
  featured on our [Sponsors page](@/sponsors.md).

Whatever the amount, your support directly helps make hardware design more open
and productive. Thank you for supporting Veryl.
